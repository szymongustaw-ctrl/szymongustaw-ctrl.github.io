Why IT

Why I chose IT

I chose IT because I like technology, solving problems and creating things. I am curious about how computers work and how they can be used to solve problems. My interest in IT started when I was a child. My dad inspired me by showing me computer hardware and introducing me to the world of technology. This helped me become interested in computers.

At school I was often the "PC guy" who helped when something did not work on computers in class room. I also have experience programming in Python which made me more interested, in software development. I want to improve my skills by creating projects and learning independently. I know that programming takes practice, problem-solving skills and patience and I want to keep developing these abilities. In the future I would like to become a software developer preferably working in backend developer.

Future me

In four years, I see myself working as a backend developer in a company where I can gain a lot of experience and improve my programming skills. I would like to learn from experienced developers. I want to work on real projects and understand how professional software is built. I also want to learn new technologies and become more indepentet as a developer. My goal is to become a backend developer in future.
