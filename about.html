About Me

Curious person with creative ideas, and a passion for technology.

Who am I?

Hello. My name is Szymon Grześkowiak, and I am from Poland. I came to the Netherlands to study HBO-ICT at HZ University of Applied Sciences. I am a curious person who enjoys learning new things, solving problems, and paying attention to details.

What do I do?

Outside of school, I spend a lot of my free time playing video games with my friends. I also enjoy learning new skills, such as video and photo editing. I am still a beginner, but I know that I can improve with more practice. I am also interested in many other subjects, including finance and astrophysics. In general, I like exploring different topics whenever something catches my attention.

What I am good at?

One of my strengths is learning. I have gained knowledge in different fields, and I enjoy understanding how new things work. I am also very good at competitive games and have reached the highest rank in several of them. Besides gaming, I like exploring different subjects and finding out more about things that interest me. I think having different interests helps me see things from different perspectives and discover new opportunities.

Why I chose ICT?

I chose HBO-ICT because I prefer learning by doing instead of focusing mostly on theory. From my previous experience, I know that I learn better when I can put things into practice. I have been interested in IT since I was a kid, and my dad also played a part in that. Now I want to learn more about computers and programming and see which part of IT I enjoy the most.

My Characteristics

  • Curious - I like learning new things and exploring different topics.
  • Problem-solving - I enjoy finding solutions to different problems.
  • Detail-oriented - I try to be precise and pay attention to details.
  • Quick learner - I enjoy learning new skills and understanding how things work.
  • Open-minded - I am interested in many different subjects.
  • Competitive - I enjoy challenges and trying to achieve the best results.
