My SWOT Table and SMART Goals

| Szymon Grześkowiak |Created on 16.10.2026 |

SWOT Table:

This table shows my strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats that I am aware of. Thanks to this table, I can improve myself and relize what I still need to work on to become better.

Strengths

I can work independently

I learn from my mistakes

I understand code without difficulty

Weaknesses

I sometimes misunderstand assignment instructions

I tend to leave my assignments until the last minute

Opportunities

I can learn from the HZ Learn and lectures

I can ask lecturers and classmates for help

Threats

I may fall behind if I do not manage my time well

I may have problem catching up after missing couple lectures

SMART 1 Strength: I can work independently

S - Specific I want to improve my ability to work independently.
M - Measurable I will make one small project every week
A - Achievable I will start with small projects
R - Relevant This will help me become a more independent programmer.
T - Time-bound I will do this for the next month.

SMART 2 Weakness: I tend to leave my assignments until the last minute

S - Specific I want to start my assignments earlier
M - Measurable I will start each assignment at least three days after its starts
A - Achievable I will start by planning the tasks I need to complete
R - Relevant This will help me manage my project work and time better
T - Time-bound I will follow this plan during the next study block

My mission this year

My mission this year is to become a more independent programmer and improve my IT skills. I will work on one small project every week to practice my solving problem abillity. I also want to enhance my time management by starting all my assignments earlier and planning them better. By following these goals, I hope to become a better student and programmer.
